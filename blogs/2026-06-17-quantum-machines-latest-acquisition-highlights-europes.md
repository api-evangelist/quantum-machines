Quantum Machines' acquisition of the Hungarian firm PCB Engineering establishes a new R&D hub in Budapest and reflects an industry-wide shift toward engineering talent and hardware expertise as quantum computing moves from lab demonstrations to commercial productization. The piece notes Quantum Machines now employs people across 22 countries and quotes CTO Yonatan Cohen on the conversation shifting toward productization, following its earlier acquisition of the Dutch QHarbor team.
